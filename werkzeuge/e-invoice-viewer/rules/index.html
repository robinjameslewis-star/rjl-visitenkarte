E-invoice validation rules explained

What messages such as BR-DE-15, BR-CO-26 or PEPPOL-EN16931-R010 mean – and what the issuer needs to change.

Validators report errors in e-invoices with numbers such as BR-DE-15. Here you find, for every rule, what is wrong and what to change, plus the original rule text. Check your own invoice free of charge in your browser, without uploading it – the tool shows every finding with this explanation.
