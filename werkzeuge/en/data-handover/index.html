Take your data to smartformular.ai

SmartFormular E-Invoice Start (formerly E-Rechnung lite) and SmartFormular Bookkeeping Start (formerly FiBu lite) have moved to smartformular.ai. If you used the tools here, your settings and sign-in are stored in this browser under robin.vision.

How it works: click “Take my data with me”. smartformular.ai opens; choose “Receive data” there. The data goes directly from window to window inside your browser, never through a server and never in an address. Nothing happens without a click.
