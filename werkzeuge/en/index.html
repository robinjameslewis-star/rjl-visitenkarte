SmartFormular FiBu

For the current month: FiBu turns bank statements and documents into the checked booking batch – for bank, payables and receivables – and links it to your open items. FiBu takes the searching and retyping off your hands; you check and decide.

  1. 01
    Post the bank accountBank statements as PDF, scan, CAMT, MT940 or CSV become the booking batch for the bank account – following your posting style. FiBu links them to your open-item lists: which payment settles which item, what remains open, where an invoice is missing.
  2. 02
    Read and match documentsInvoices and credit notes as PDF or e-invoice: FiBu reads amount, date, tax rate and invoice number and matches the payment – including early-payment discounts and part payments.
  3. 03
    Post payables and receivablesPurchase and sales invoices become the booking batch on the supplier and customer accounts. Using your master data and account list, FiBu assigns each document to the right supplier or customer; for new business partners it proposes a new account for you to decide on.
  4. 04
    Your posting style per clientContra account, tax key, cost centre and posting text follow the last twelve months per business partner – as a file kept with you, no model training.
  5. 05
    A clarification list instead of guessworkAnything ambiguous becomes a question you answer with one click. Every line shows its origin – traceable and auditable under the German GoBD.
  6. 06
    Estimate VATFor the German advance VAT return: you choose accrual or cash accounting, or your legal form, and FiBu works out from bank statements and documents the expected VAT payable or the excess input VAT to be refunded – by rate, with the origin of every amount. An estimate to prepare the return; you or your firm still prepare and file it.

SmartFormular Deklaration

The working paper that thinks ahead and does the groundwork.

For year-end accounts in the firm. Deklaration does the groundwork every set of accounts needs – from the prior-year comparison to the briefing for the year-end meeting. Every suggestion comes with its derivation, every decision stays with you. The result is a set of proposed closing entries and the preparation of the German e-balance sheet.

  1. 01
    Prior-year figures and movementsEvery item compared with the prior year, plus movement schedules – fixed assets, provisions, loans, equity – from the trial balance and postings.
  2. 02
    Reconcile loansInterest and repayments against the repayment schedule, opening and closing balances against the agreement and the bank confirmation. Differences are ready as clarification points.
  3. 03
    Review open itemsOpen receivables and payables against the trial balance, settlements after the balance sheet date, duplicates and items posted to the wrong account.
  4. 04
    Long-outstanding receivables and payablesWhatever has been open for years is found and prepared for valuation: age, last movement, a proposal for a write-down or write-off. The decision stays with the professional.
  5. 05
    Calculate provisionsYear-end and audit costs, document retention, holiday and overtime, taxes – with the calculation path and the roll-forward from the prior year.
  6. 06
    Put the figures in proportionRevenue, gross profit, cost of materials, staff costs, profit and other key items as ratios, compared with the prior year and with the anomalies worth discussing.
  7. 07
    Prepare the year-end meetingA briefing for the client: the key figures, changes on the prior year, open questions and the decisions that rest with the client.

Deklaration is software for your firm, not tax advice. File, deadlines, sign-off and transmission stay in your firm's software.

The principle

People and machine

SmartFormular does not post and does not declare. It prepares, marks the certainty of every line – certain, suggestion, clarification – and hands over only after your review. Quality and pace rise because your team searches less and decides more.

  • Suggestions with their origin
  • A clarification list instead of silent errors
  • Finalised in your software
  • Judgement with the professional

Open to every accounting package

Not just DATEV: SmartFormular is being built to exchange data with every accounting package – importing and exporting, starting in Germany and in the long run worldwide. You keep your software; SmartFormular works alongside it.

  1. 01
    Import and exportBank statements, documents, master data and account lists, booking batches, open items and trial balances come in from your software; the results go back in the format your software imports.
  2. 02
    A neutral data modelInternally SmartFormular works with its own data model, independent of any package. Each package is added through its own connector – by file or interface, interfaces read-only at first. Your work in SmartFormular stays the same.
  3. 03
    From Germany to the worldFirst the packages that firms and companies in German-speaking countries use, with their charts of accounts. Then further countries with their charts of accounts, tax rules and standards for bank data and e-invoices.

Today FiBu lite exports booking batches in the DATEV format, which many programs import. Which packages are connected next depends on the firms and companies in the beta – tell me what you use.

Language model: optional and under your control

  1. 01
    OptionalSmartFormular works with rules. A language model is added only where rules fall short – reading references, contracts and resolutions, or drafting texts. Calculating, reconciling and judging stay with rules and people.
  2. 02
    No training on your dataYour data does not train a model. The program gives the model fixed instructions per task and checks every output – schema, totals, accounts.
  3. 03
    In the EU, no lock-inThe model runs with a European provider of your choice or locally on your systems. The program shows which provider is involved; data leaves pseudonymised or not at all.

Questions and answers

What is the difference between FiBu lite and SmartFormular FiBu?

FiBu lite posts bank statements by the previous month's pattern – free, offline. FiBu additionally reads documents, posts payables and receivables, links them to your open items, keeps the clarification list and shows the origin of every line.

Do I need an AI model?

No. SmartFormular works with rules. A model can be added to improve suggestions where rules fall short – in the EU, without training on your data.

Which software does SmartFormular work with?

The aim is to exchange data with every accounting package – importing and exporting, starting in Germany and in the long run worldwide. Each package is added through its own connector. Today FiBu lite exports booking batches in the DATEV format, which many programs import; at launch FiBu reads open-item lists from DATEV and ADDISON, with further packages following during the beta. Deklaration works alongside your firm's software.

Does FiBu calculate the advance VAT return?

FiBu estimates it: on an accrual or cash basis from bank statements and documents, with the VAT payable or the excess input VAT and the origin of every amount. Anything unclear, such as a missing invoice, goes on the clarification list. It is preparation, not tax advice; the return is filed with your own software or by your firm.

Does SmartFormular replace accounting staff?

No. It takes over searching and retyping and works out connections. Checking, deciding and finalising stay with your team.

What does SmartFormular cost?

FiBu lite is free. Beta access to FiBu and Deklaration is free of charge; prices for the finished products come with the quote.
