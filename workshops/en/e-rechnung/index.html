E-invoicing workshop (in German)

Self-study, about 45 minutes, in your own browser

Since 2025, businesses in Germany must be able to receive e-invoices; from 2027 and 2028 they must also issue them. In about 45 minutes, this workshop shows what an e-invoice is, how to receive, read and check one, and what applies to your business and when. You finish with your own roadmap to 2028.

  • Right in your browser, whenever it suits you, at your own pace
  • Videos with Goch, self-checks and an exercise with realistic sample invoices and model solutions
  • For your team: add colleagues when booking, each person gets their own access. If you like, show it together on a projector or in a call.
  • Checklist, a card “Questions for your software” and a certificate of attendance for each person

Free until 7 January 2027 (launch phase, 30 days access from booking).
