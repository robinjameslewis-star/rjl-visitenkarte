AI literacy workshop for your team

With EU AI Act training

A workshop that lowers barriers. An online course that consolidates knowledge. A certificate that documents your measure under Article 4 of the EU AI Act.

For accounting firms and companies · online or on site, across Europe · in German or English · dates from January 2027

Artificial intelligence has arrived in your team – in the inbox, in bookkeeping, in everyday work. The EU AI Act therefore requires every organisation that uses AI to take measures to ensure the AI literacy of its staff. This workshop is one such measure: practical, tailored to accounting and finance, with evidence.

Your team learns what AI can do today and where it fails, which data must never go into a public tool and how to check results before they count. At the end there is a certificate for each person – and the confidence to use AI properly in daily work.

How it works

  1. You send an enquiry with your company, the number of participants, a preferred date and the format. You receive a quote and proposed dates, usually within two working days.
  2. Once you accept, I set up your access in the member area. You enter your participants; each person receives an invitation and their own access – with a passkey, no password.

  4. The test completes it: 15 questions, every answer explained, repeatable at any time. On passing, the certificate is issued as a PDF – with a number and a verification code.
  5. You download the participant list for your training records. Access lasts 90 days; the certificates remain verifiable for three years.

Evidence and certificate

The certificate states the person, the organisation, the content, duration, date, edition and legal position of the training, plus its number and verification code. It is evidence issued by robin.vision of a measure under Article 4 of the EU AI Act – not an official attestation, and it says so.

Any certificate can be verified at member.robin.vision/zertifikat. As the employer you see each person's status – invited, started, course completed, passed – and the certificate number.

Legal framework

Article 4 of the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) obliges providers and deployers of AI systems to take measures that promote the AI literacy of their staff. Which measures is for the organisation to decide; a particular format, a test or a certificate is not prescribed. This workshop is one such measure – with evidence that shows what you have done.

The workshop explains obligations in general terms, with the legal position stated for each edition. It is not legal advice; for individual cases it refers to tax advisers, lawyers and data protection officers. Your contracting partner is Robin James Lewis, trading as robin.vision; the terms of the member area apply.

Questions and answers

Is this training mandatory?

The EU AI Act requires organisations that use AI to take measures to ensure the AI literacy of their staff – how is for the organisation to decide. This workshop is one such measure, with evidence.

Why a workshop and not just an online course?

Because barriers fall in conversation and questions from your own daily work help most. In the workshop your team develops its own use cases and builds a small tool; the course consolidates, the test completes.

What is the tool we build?

A single HTML file that runs in the browser and eases one concrete task of your team – checking a list, generating a text by fixed rules, summarising figures. No installation, no data in the cloud.

What do I see as the employer?

Each person's status – invited, started, course completed, passed – and the certificate number, as a list for your training records. Scores and attempts are not shown.

What happens if someone fails the test?

After every submission the correct answer and an explanation are shown for each question. The test can be repeated as often as needed, with freshly drawn questions.

What do we need technically?

A browser and an email address per person. Access is set up with a passkey, for example by fingerprint. The course runs in the browser, on a phone too.

Are products shown in the workshop?

Yes, on real cases: robin.vision's own products and selected tools from partner companies, labelled as partner offers. The content of the training is not affected; what you use is your decision.

Is the workshop legal advice?

No. It explains obligations in general terms and refers to tax advisers, lawyers and data protection officers for individual cases.
