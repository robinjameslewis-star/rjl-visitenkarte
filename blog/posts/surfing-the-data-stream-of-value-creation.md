You already know it from integrated business software (ERP) and digital HR management with an employee portal (HRMS with ESS): master data and transaction data are made available for further processing as they arise, either in real time or at defined intervals. ERP systems have been doing this for invoicing for decades. The invoicing data is available as a record for further processing, for example in accounts receivable. In this process, the image of the invoice has never mattered for the result. It was only needed by the invoice recipient and by the tax auditors. That reason for the invoice image to exist is now gone for good. Eureka! That is why I am so delighted about the current changes in German tax law that everyone is talking about under the heading of e-invoicing. As a bookkeeper, I have spent long enough translating invoice images into data! Those days are over.
