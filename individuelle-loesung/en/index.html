Custom solution

You come to me with a problem or an idea. I turn it into the solution that fits, from a small helper for one step of your work to a market-ready product.

  1. Stage 1 Small solution
  2. Stage 2 Market-ready product

Stage 1

Small solution

What it is

You describe a problem from your working day. I build a solution that fits, created with AI and checked by me before you receive it. One round of changes is included.

For example:

  • Clean drawings from sketches
  • Merging and checking spreadsheets
  • Building templates and forms
  • Organising and designing documents

How it works

  1. You describe the problemWhat you need, what it is for and in what form. To begin with, send only the description, no documents yet.
  2. You receive a quote firstI check whether and how I can solve the task and ask if anything is unclear. Then you receive a quote.
  3. You get the checked solutionOnce you accept, I create the solution with AI and check it myself. You receive the result and one round of changes.

Who it is for

For small businesses, the self-employed and private individuals with a one-off problem involving documents, data or design. Accounting firms are welcome to ask too, as long as no client data is involved.

Stage 2

Market-ready product

For the open market or for your team and business: your idea or problem becomes a product of its own, with everything that goes with it.

The building blocks

  1. Idea to concept

    Together we sharpen your idea or problem into a brief and a concept, with costs worked out and risks stated honestly.

  2. Name and brand

    You receive name suggestions with domain and trade mark searches, and a design of your own with colours, imagery and, if you wish, a character of your own.

  3. Website

    Your website in German and English with real depth, legal pages, contact form, appointment booking and newsletter, checked on a phone.

  4. Personal assistant

    A character on your website answers questions about what you offer, with a voice if you wish, and with fixed cost limits.

  5. Paymentfrom January 2027

    Customers pay directly on your site via Stripe, with confirmation by email.

  6. Customer or staff portalfrom January 2027

    Your customers or your team sign in with an email link and see orders, invoices and files.

  7. Accountingfrom January 2027

    Every payment becomes an invoice, and your tax adviser receives the month as an export.

  8. Getting found

    Search engines find your site because it is technically sound, and there is a starter pack for your newsletter and LinkedIn.

Everything belongs to you from the start: your own domain, your own accounts, ready to hand over.

This is how my own brands, each with its own website, came about. Here too, it starts with your description, and you receive a quote first.

What I don't do

  • no tax or legal advice, and nothing that requires a special licence
  • no client data from accounting firms and no health data
  • no official documents, planning applications, structural calculations, expert reports or valuations
  • nothing intended to deceive, such as receipts, references or reviews
  • no tax advice in the accounting module: it creates invoices and the export for your tax adviser

Every solution is created with AI and checked by me. It is not an official document and does not replace professional advice. Please check it before you use it.

Tell me about your task

What keeps holding you up at work, or which idea would you like to bring to market? Describe it in a few sentences. This is the kind of thing it can become:

  • A searchable overview from a stack of as-built plans
  • A form that checks entries before they go into your system
  • An order intake that checks everything is there, passes the job to the right person and prepares the work for them
  • A template that turns your data into a finished report

Please describe the task, not the contents: don't send client data, health data or any real personal data.
